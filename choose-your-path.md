title: Choose your path
---

Pick **one** part and follow it from start to finish. You don't need to read the others.

| Your computer | Follow | Start here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Windows 10 (version 2004 or later) or Windows 11 | **Part 1: Windows (WSL)** | [](windows/before-you-start.md) |
| Mac (Apple Silicon or Intel) | **Part 2: Mac** | [](mac/before-you-start.md) |
| A locked-down machine (work laptop, no admin rights), a Chromebook, or a local setup you can't get working | **Part 3: GCP Cloud Shell** | [](cloud-shell/before-you-start.md) |

:::{important} Cloud Shell is the fallback
Use Windows or Mac if you can. Cloud Shell works entirely in your browser and needs no installs, but it has usage limits and resets everything outside your home folder (see [](cloud-shell/before-you-start.md)). It's the right choice when a local setup isn't possible, and a good backup if your laptop breaks mid-semester.
:::

:::{note} Not sure whether you have admin rights?
On Windows, the first real step is running PowerShell **as administrator**. On a Mac, the first install asks for your Mac password. If you can't do either, switch to Part 3.
:::
